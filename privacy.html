Privacy Policy – Necroscope Uploader

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Necroscope Uploader is a personal tool used only by the owner of "The Necroscope". It runs only on the owner's computer and has no servers of its own.

What it accesses from TikTok: basic profile information (display name and an account identifier), to show which account is connected, and permission to upload videos to the owner's drafts.

What it stores: the login tokens TikTok provides, kept in a file on the owner's computer that only the owner can read. The video clips it sends are made on that computer from the owner's own music, and are deleted locally once uploaded.

Sharing: no data is sold, shared or sent anywhere other than to TikTok, as part of the upload.

Removing access: the owner can disconnect the tool at any time in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions) and delete the stored tokens from their computer.
